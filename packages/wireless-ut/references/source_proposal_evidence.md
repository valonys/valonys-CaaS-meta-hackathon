## Limitations and assurance boundary

- Images alone cannot establish remaining wall thickness, corrosion rate, fitness-for-service or inspection acceptance. No chart values have been digitized as measurements.
- Final decisions require calibrated UT data, equipment validation, approved inspection procedures and competent-person review. Single-point monitoring does not prove the condition of unmeasured areas or replace a spatial scan.
- Numerical savings, service-life extensions, installation times, network performance and temperature limits in slides are presentation claims, not verified design inputs. Do not use them as committed benefits or acceptance criteria.
- Sampling can miss brief slides and animations. Scene detection uses 2 fps reduced-resolution analysis; camera-view switches can also score as changes. The complete video and audio remain the context record.
- Some frames contain overlays, small text or existing webcam/name displays. No people were identified and no personal information was inferred or transcribed. Only clear selected headings were captured by OCR and visual checking.
- Source frames are 1640 × 914, suitable for review and small poster panels; they do not become high-resolution print assets by enlargement. Obtain original diagrams or approved redraws for large-format production.
- Site, asset list, material, damage mechanisms, hazardous-area classification, calibration, uncertainty, raw UT signals, cost basis and deployment results remain unverified.
